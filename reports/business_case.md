# Corporate Finance Business Case: FP&A Rolling Forecast Engine

## 1. Executive Summary & Investment Thesis
Upgrading corporate financial planning from manual spreadsheet run-rates to an automated, driver-based rolling forecast engine directly impacts operating margins, working capital efficiency, and supply chain scheduling costs.

## 3. Benefit Quantification Methodology

### A. Working Capital Optimization ($2.10M in Base Case)
- Total revenue across 8 operating divisions averages ~$2.9B annually.
- Safety stock and liquidity reserves currently scale to cover forecast uncertainty ($\sim 10.5\%$ error).
- Improving forecast accuracy by 3.8 points liberates an estimated $21M in unproductive safety inventory. At a 10% cost of capital, this releases $2.10M in annualized cash flow savings.

### B. Expediting Cost Mitigation ($850k in Base Case)
- Production schedules set with inaccurate demand forecasts routinely trigger expedited freight, overtime premiums, and supplier change orders.
- Historical operations records indicate $3.2M spent annually on logistics expediting; a 26% reduction yields $850k in direct cost avoidance.

### C. Labor Productivity & Capacity ($120k in Base Case)
- 8 divisional FP&A teams spend 15 hours each month manually updating Excel models.
- Automation saves 1,200 hours annually, redirected toward business partnering, pricing strategy, and contract negotiations.
